Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Habitua ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Habitua is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • Data you create: Habit names, descriptions, reminder times, completion history, streaks, and rewards data. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Device data: We do not collect personal identification data. The app may use device identifiers only in connection with advertising (see Third-Party Services below).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide habit tracking, reminders, and reward features.
  • To display notifications (e.g., daily reminders) when you have granted permission.
  • To improve the app experience and fix issues.

3. Local Storage

Your habits, logs, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to our servers unless you use a feature that explicitly syncs data (if offered in the future).

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data (such as device ID, advertising ID, and usage data) in accordance with:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy & Messaging

In regions where required, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent for personalized ads. You can change your consent choices in your device or Google account settings.

5. Permissions

We request only the permissions necessary for the app to function:

  • Notifications: To send reminder notifications for your habits.
  • Exact alarm / Schedule exact alarm: To trigger reminders at the exact time you set (e.g., daily habit reminders).
  • Receive boot completed: To reschedule reminders after your device restarts.
  • Vibration: To vibrate when a reminder notification is shown.
  • Internet: Used by the ad network to display ads; the core app works offline.

6. Data Security

Data stored on your device is held in the app's private storage. We do not transmit your habit or completion data to our servers. We recommend keeping your device locked and using a secure backup if you back up app data.

7. Children's Privacy

Habitua is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access or receive a copy of your data (your data is in the app on your device).
  • Delete your data (e.g., by clearing app data or uninstalling the app).
  • Opt out of personalized advertising via your device or Google account settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the app or on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
